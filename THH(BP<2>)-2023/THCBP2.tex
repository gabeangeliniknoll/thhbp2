\section{Topological Hochschild cohomology of $BP\langle n\rangle$}
We will write $\THC(BP\langle n\rangle ; M)$ for the topological Hochschild cohomology of $BP\langle 2\rangle$ with coefficients in a $\tBP{n}^e:=\tBP{n}\wedge \tBP{n}^{\op}$-module $M$, which is defined to be
\[ 
\THC(\BP\langle n\rangle;M) :=  F_{\BP\langle n\rangle^e} (\BP\langle n\rangle,M) \,. 
\]
By adjunction, we can identify
\[\THC(\BP\langle n\rangle)=F_{\BP\langle n\rangle} (\THH(\BP\langle n\rangle),\BP\langle n\rangle),. 
\]
By~\cite{AHL10}, we know that there is an isomorphism of Hopf algebras
\[ \THC^*(\ell;\mathbb{Z}_{(p)})=\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}\langle x_{2p-1}\rangle\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}}\Gamma(c_{1})/(pc_{1})
\]
where $x_{2p-1}$ is dual to $\lambda_1$ and $|x_{2p-1}|=-2p+1$ and $c_1$ is dual to $\mu^{p^2}$ and $|c_1|=-2p^2$. 
There is a $v_1$-Bockstein spectral sequence 
\[
\THC^*(\ell;\mathbb{Z}_{(p)})[v_1]\implies  \THC^*(\ell) \,.
\]
Note that already this implies that $\THC^*(\ell)$ is concentrated in degrees congruent to $-2$, $-1$ and $0$ modulo $2p-2$. In particular, the class $c_1$ must survive the spectral sequence determining 
\[ 
\THC^{-2p^2}(\ell)\cong \mathbb{Z}/p\{c_1\}
\]
We compute the cap product 
\[\THC^{k}(\ell)\otimes \THH_{m}(\ell)\to \THH_{m}(\ell)
\]
satisfies  
\[ 
c_1\cap b_n=(n-1)b_{n-1}
\]
as in~\cite[Corollary~5.7]{AHL10}.
